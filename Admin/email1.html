Respected Sir/Madam,

My name is Hafiz Abid Hussain, an Overseas Pakistani residing in the United Kingdom. I am writing with regard to my complaint concerning the lack of a sewage system in Village Mahmood Abad, P.O. Fathepur, District Gujrat, where 3 to 4 individuals are refusing to allow a pipeline to be laid to the nearby Abinala. This matter was referred by your office to the Provincial Mohtasib Punjab on 14 September 2026 and registered as Case No. WMS-ONL/0061314/26, with my affidavit submitted.

Not a Single Response

Over more than a month, I have sent numerous emails to the Mohtasib offices, to every officer, department, administrative contact and email address I have been given. Not a single officer, office, assistant or staff member has replied to any of them, or responded to my attempts to make contact. Not even one. I have not received so much as an acknowledgement of receipt, a status update, or the name of a person handling my case.

This is deeply disappointing. The Mohtasib institution exists to protect citizens from maladministration, yet I, a citizen who has followed every instruction and procedure, have been met with complete silence. In the meantime, the sewage water in my village remains stagnant, the schoolchildren still have to walk through contaminated water, and with winter and heavy rain approaching, the risk of disease and hardship for local residents is growing every day. The other government offices I have contacted have also failed to act, which is why this case matters so much.

I would like a straight answer: is there any point in my continuing to email your offices, or should I simply give up?

I would be grateful if your office could:

1. Confirm receipt of this email and acknowledge Case No. WMS-ONL/0061314/26.
2. Provide the name and direct contact details of the officer responsible for my case at the Provincial Mohtasib Punjab, Lahore.
3. Direct the Provincial Mohtasib office to respond to me and provide an update on the case without further delay.
4. Look into why none of the Mohtasib offices has responded to my repeated emails.
5. Provide a definite timeline for action, given the approach of winter.
6. Contact me directly on my UK phone number or by email.

I have copies of all my emails, along with photographic and video evidence of the site conditions, which I am ready to provide at any time. I sincerely hope this email will finally receive a reply.

Thank you for your time and consideration.
